About the extension

Play an Nakiri Ayame noise directly from your browser! It is an amazing way to enrich your browser experience, by utilizing simple concepts you instantly get your Nakirum dosage for the day using Ayame Sounds. It's just that easy. Install, Click and enjoy the experience. Recently in version 1.2 we've added seven whole new voicelines bringing the total sounds to 58! That's right fifty-eight sounds to immerse yourself in Ojou-sama's heavenly sounds.

How to use

To use "Ayame Noises" use this step-by-step guide:

  1. Install the extension from the Chrome Webstore or Firefox Add-ons
  2. Click on the extension icon in the toolbar
  3. Enjoy the sound!
